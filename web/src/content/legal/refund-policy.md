# GmailSmith — Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

This policy explains refunds and cancellations for paid GmailSmith plans. It
applies to consumers and business buyers. It does not limit any mandatory
rights you have under the law that applies to you.

## Who sells GmailSmith

GmailSmith is developed and operated by an individual developer based in
Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. GmailSmith is the seller name used for Paddle.

Paid orders are processed by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of
Record. Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com.
Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all
customer service inquiries and handles returns.

When you buy a paid plan, your payment transaction is with Paddle, not with
GmailSmith. Paddle handles payment, invoicing, applicable taxes, subscription
renewals, cancellations, and refunds. Paddle's
[Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) and
[Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) also apply to that
transaction.

## What this policy covers

This policy covers paid **Personal** and **Agency** subscriptions, whether
billed monthly or annually, purchased through Paddle.

The **Free** plan does not involve payment, so no refund applies to it.

This policy does not cover charges from Google or Gmail, Google Workspace,
third-party email services, or other products and services that GmailSmith
does not sell.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on the first paid charge for a new
Personal or Agency subscription. If you are not satisfied, you may request a
full refund of that first paid charge within 30 days of the Paddle transaction
date. You do not need to give a reason.

The guarantee applies once to the first paid charge for a new subscription. It
does not apply to later renewal charges, which are covered by the renewal
window below.

## Renewal charges

You may request a full refund of a renewal charge within **14 days** of that
renewal's Paddle transaction date.

After that period, the renewal is not eligible for a change-of-mind refund.
This does not affect any remedy you have for a service defect, a charge made in
error, or a right that applicable law does not allow us to limit.

## Cancelling a subscription

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future billing and takes effect
at the end of the billing period you have already paid for. You keep access
until then. Cancellation does not by itself refund amounts already paid.

You can cancel through:

- the **Manage subscription** link in your Paddle confirmation email;
- the Paddle customer portal or [paddle.net](https://paddle.net); or
- our support email, and we will help you find the right Paddle route.

## Monthly and annual plans

**Monthly plans.** The 30-day guarantee applies to the first monthly charge.
A renewal charge can be refunded within 14 days of that renewal's transaction
date. After the window, we do not provide partial-month refunds. Cancel to stop the next
month; access continues to the end of the current paid month.

**Annual plans.** The 30-day guarantee applies to the first annual charge.
A renewal charge can be refunded within 14 days of that renewal's transaction
date. After the window, we do not provide pro-rata refunds for unused months. Cancel before the
renewal date to avoid the next annual charge; access continues to the end of
the paid annual term.

## Plan changes

Paddle handles upgrades, downgrades, and any proration, and shows the resulting
charge at checkout. A charge for a plan change follows the same first-charge or
renewal rule that applies to that charge. We do not provide a refund for unused
time after the applicable window.

## Duplicate, erroneous, or unauthorised charges

If you see a duplicate charge, a charge made in error, or a charge you did not
authorise, contact us and Paddle promptly. We will investigate with Paddle. A
verified duplicate or erroneous charge is refunded in full. For an unauthorised
charge, also contact your payment provider.

## Service defects or misdescription

If a verified defect in GmailSmith prevents you from using the core paid
features, contact support first and give us a reasonable opportunity to fix it.
If we cannot resolve the problem within a reasonable time, we will provide a
refund or another remedy as required by law. This can apply outside the normal
refund window.

## How to request a refund

The fastest route is through Paddle, because Paddle is the Merchant of Record:

- use the **View receipt** or **Manage subscription** link in your Paddle
  confirmation email;
- visit [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) and choose the refund option; or
- email **support@gmailsmith.app** and we will help you with the Paddle route.

Please include:

- the email address used for the purchase;
- your Paddle order or invoice number, if you have it;
- the plan name; and
- a short reason, if you want to give one.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within two business days. Do not send
card numbers, passwords, or other payment credentials by email.

## How refunds are processed

Paddle processes approved refunds to the original payment method where
possible, normally within 14 days of approval. Your bank or payment provider
may take additional time to show the refund. Refunds include applicable taxes
paid to Paddle; Paddle handles the tax treatment.

We do not deduct any fees from an approved refund.

## What happens after a refund

When a refund is approved, the subscription is cancelled, paid access ends,
and any licence key issued for that subscription is deactivated. GmailSmith
remains available on the Free plan.

Refunds and cancellations do not delete the campaigns, CSV files, attachments,
history, or other data stored on your computer. Export anything you need before
the paid access ends. The Privacy Policy explains how to delete local data.

## Fraud and refund abuse

Paddle may refuse a refund where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse, or
manipulative behaviour. Repeated subscribe-and-refund cycles may be treated as
abuse. This does not limit any right you have under applicable law.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits a right or remedy that applicable law
does not allow us to exclude or limit.

If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA or the UK, you may have a statutory right to
withdraw from a digital purchase within 14 days. Where the law gives you a
longer period or an additional remedy, that longer period or remedy applies.
Our 30-day guarantee on the first paid charge is in addition to those rights.

Paddle's [Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) and
[Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) also apply to the
